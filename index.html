Our technology

Built on real-world AI.

ISL isn’t English, word for word. It has its own grammar, space and rhythm — so SignText reads movement over time and translates meaning, not just handshapes.

  1. 01

    Camera

    Any phone camera. Processing starts on-device.

  2. 02

    MediaPipe Holistic

    Tracks 543 body, face and hand keypoints in every frame.

  3. 03

    Temporal encoder

    Reads signs as motion across frames, not still poses.

  4. 04

    Language model

    Turns ISL glosses into fluent, natural sentences.

  5. 05

    Your output

    Text, speech or captions — wherever you need them.
